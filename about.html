More about me

I’m the kind of person who gets curious about everything—how something works, why it was built that way, what would happen if you swapped a piece out. Lately most of that curiosity is aimed at AI tools; I love pulling them apart, prompting them into corners, and figuring out where they actually help versus where they just sound clever.

On the creative side, I dabble in video editing and graphic design. Most of that lives on my personal Instagram — @randomviewsof21yearold.

Before all this, at 17, I was a part-time Twitch streamer—Fortnite, Valorant, Rocket League, whatever was fun that week. I also moderated a US streamer’s channel for about a year, which is where I first learned how communities really run behind the scenes.

I finished my Bachelor’s in Business Management at Christ University in 2025 while juggling all of this. It gave me the foundation, the friendships, and a bit of polish to go with it.
